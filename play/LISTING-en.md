## Full description (up to 4000 characters)

Domiday is a private journal of your trips and of where you are now. It answers “when and where was I”, counts your days by country, and keeps everything on your phone only.

I built this app for myself, and it turned out useful enough to share. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

NO DOMIDAY ACCOUNT. NO SERVER. NO INTERNET ACCESS
The app has no internet permission and does not upload your journal to a server. There is nothing to sign up for. Your journal lives in the app’s private folder; you save a backup as a single file wherever you like, and sharing a picture or a file happens only when you choose to.

WHAT IT DOES
• Today: how many days you have been in the current country, “On this day” in past years, four tiles for the year, the next planned trip and your latest trips.
• Year calendar: every day painted with its country colour; transit and a day in two countries are marked separately. Expanded month with country codes, a day card. Filter by country.
• Map and a rotating globe: visited countries in political-map colours, search that flies to a country, “12 of 250”, all time or one year. Disputed territories are assigned to no one, as on a neutral map.
• Journal: trips grouped by year with flags, cities, dates and days. Search by country, city, note and year. A page for every country.
• Trip plans: future dates show in the journal and the calendar but do not count until you confirm the trip. When the day comes, the app asks: “Are you there?”
• Year in review: countries and new countries, days at home and away, the longest trip, the map, cities — and a picture you can share.
• Google Maps Timeline import: countries are matched offline, you add only the periods you want.
• Country detection on your tap and when the app opens: an approximate location is matched against borders stored inside the app; coordinates are never saved. It is a suggestion that you confirm. Background checks are off by default.
• Backup and restore, “Clear everything” with confirmation, English and Russian, light and dark themes.

HONEST COUNTING RULES
Arrival and departure days both count. Overlapping days count once. A moving day belongs to both countries. Unknown days are not home days. The current year counts through today.

WHAT DOMIDAY IS NOT
It is a journal of facts, not a visa or tax calculator. It does not determine residency, entry eligibility or visa limits.

Country borders: Natural Earth, public domain. Requires 64-bit Android 7.0 or later.
