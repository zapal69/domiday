## Full description (up to 4000 characters)

Domiday is a travel days counter and a private travel journal. It counts how many days you have spent in each country, shows your trips on a calendar, a map and a rotating globe, and keeps everything on your phone only. No account, no server, no ads.

WHY A TRAVEL DAYS COUNTER
If you live abroad, work remotely or simply travel a lot, sooner or later you need an exact number: how many days in this country this year, how many at home, how many away. Residency rules, visa limits and tax questions all come down to days. Domiday keeps that record for you with clear counting rules, so the number is ready when you need it.

TRACK DAYS IN EVERY COUNTRY
• Today: how many days you have been in the current country, “On this day” in past years, four tiles for the year, the next planned trip and your latest trips.
• Year calendar: every day painted with its country colour; transit and a day in two countries are marked separately. Expanded month with country codes, a day card for any date. Filter by country.
• Journal: trips grouped by year with flags, cities, dates and days. Search by country, city, note and year. A page for every country you have visited.
• Trip plans: future dates show in the journal and the calendar but do not count until you confirm the trip. When the day comes, the app asks: “Are you there?”

SEE YOUR TRAVELS ON A MAP AND A GLOBE
• Visited countries in political-map colours, search that flies to a country, “12 of 250”, all time or one year.
• A rotating offline globe with your countries on it. Disputed territories are assigned to no one, as on a neutral map.
• Year in review: countries and new countries, days at home and away, the longest trip, the map, cities — and a picture you can share.

FILL IN PAST YEARS QUICKLY
• Google Maps Timeline import: export your Timeline, open the file in Domiday, countries are matched offline, and you add only the periods you want. Nothing is written without your confirmation.
• Country detection on your tap and when the app opens: an approximate location is matched against borders stored inside the app; coordinates are never saved. It is a suggestion that you confirm. Background checks are off by default.

HONEST COUNTING RULES
Arrival and departure days both count. Overlapping days count once. A moving day belongs to both countries. Unknown days are never turned into home days. The current year counts through today. Plans never enter the totals.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No Domiday account and nothing to sign up for.
• No internet permission: the app cannot upload your journal anywhere.
• Your journal lives in the app’s private folder; you save a backup as a single file wherever you like.
• Sharing a picture or a file happens only when you choose to, through the standard Android share menu.
• Backup and restore, “Clear everything” with confirmation, English and Russian, light and dark themes.

WHAT DOMIDAY IS NOT
It is a journal of facts, not a visa or tax calculator. It does not determine residency, entry eligibility or visa limits — it gives you the days, you make the decisions.

ABOUT
I built this app for myself, and it turned out useful enough to share. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Country borders: Natural Earth, public domain. Requires 64-bit Android 7.0 or later.
